# HackCI

## Overview

HackCI, presented by CI Tech, is a 48-hour hackathon taking place at Ontario Tech University near the end of March.

The event is expected to host approximately 150–250 students from a variety of universities. Students are welcome to stay overnight on campus.

The budget will be handled by CI Tech and their sponsors. The final budget is still to be determined.

HackCI is a 48-hour event filled with continuous interactive activities for students. Workshops, hacking sessions, and networking events are made possible through sponsors, TAs, and mentors. The budget for meals is split between EngSoc and CI Tech.

---

## Mission

The goal of HackCI is to provide engaging activities for students with a passion for innovation.

Students are welcome to join as solo hackers or in teams.

HackCI maximizes student engagement by providing:
- Workshops
- Guidance
- Hacking sessions
- Networking sessions with companies

Students will have the opportunity to:
- Apply and develop technical skills
- Tackle real-world problems
- Network with companies such as Ontario Power Generation
- Explore co-op opportunities
- Build and deliver a project to judges
